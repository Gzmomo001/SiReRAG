Alex O'Loughlin
Alex O'Loughlin / oʊ ˈlɒklɪn / (born 24 August 1976) is an Australian actor and director, who plays Lieutenant Commander Steve McGarrett on CBS 'remake of the TV series Hawaii Five - 0. He had starring roles in the films Oyster Farmer (2004) and The Back - up Plan (2010), as well as on such television series as Moonlight (2008) and Three Rivers (2009).

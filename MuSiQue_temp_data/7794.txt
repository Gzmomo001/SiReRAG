Just One Last Time
"Just One Last Time" is a song performed by French DJ and music producer David Guetta, featuring vocals from Swedish vocal duo Taped Rai. The track was released as the second single from the re-release of "Nothing but the Beat", 2.0, and serves as the album's eighth single overall. The song was released via digital download on November 15, 2012, and was released as a CD single on February 8, 2013. The song is used as the theme song for the film "A Good Day to Die Hard" (2013).

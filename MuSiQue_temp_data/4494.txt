Push, Nevada
Created by Ben Affleck and Sean Bailey (co-producer of "Project Greenlight") and co-produced with Matt Damon and Chris Moore, "Push, Nevada" was unique in that it offered viewers a chance to follow along and solve the mystery of Push for a prize of $1,045,000. Each episode contained clues, from web addresses in the opening credits to specific phrases uttered by characters in the show, each having its own significance to the mystery.

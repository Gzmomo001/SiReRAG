Dmitri N. Smirnov (footballer)
Dmitri Nikolayevich Smirnov (; born 9 November 1980 in Moscow) is a Russian footballer. He is not related to Dmitry Alexandrovich Smirnov with whom he played on the same team for several years for FC Torpedo-ZIL Moscow, Luch and Tom. To avoid confusion, he is usually referred to as Dmitri N. Smirnov.

Bussen Trafikkselskap
Bussen Trafikkselskap was a Norwegian bus company that operated the "yellow" city bus in Kristiansand and surrounding areas Vennesla, Søgne and Songdalen. The company had about 300 employees and operates on contract with Vest-Agder Kollektivtrafikk. Nettbuss Sør (blue buses) took over the local buses in Kristiansand 01.01.2011.

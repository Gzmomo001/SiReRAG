Yuyutsu
Yuyutsu (Sanskrit: युयुत्सु) in the Hindu epic Mahabharata was a son of Dhritarashtra with Sughada / Sauvali, his wife Gandhari's maid. He was the paternal half - sibling to Gandhari's children: Duryodhana and the rest of the 99 Kuru brothers and their sister Dushala. Eventually, he was the only son of Dhritarashtra who survived the Kurukshetra war.

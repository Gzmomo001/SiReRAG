Freeway Fighter
Freeway Fighter is a single-player roleplaying gamebook written by Ian Livingstone, illustrated by Kevin Bulmer and originally published in 1985 by Puffin Books. It was later republished by Wizard Books in 2005. It forms part of Steve Jackson and Ian Livingstone's "Fighting Fantasy" series. It is the 13th in the series in the original Puffin series () and 23rd in the modern Wizard series ().

Escape Plan (video game)
Escape Plan is a puzzle video game released for the PlayStation Vita in 2012, as a launch title. "Escape Plan" is the first title from new developer Fun Bits Interactive and is produced by Chris Millar, known for his previous work on Fat Princess. In previews of the game it was noted for its sharp, black-and-white visuals, gruesome yet humorous tone and its intuitive use of the PlayStation Vita's inputs including touch and gyroscopic control.

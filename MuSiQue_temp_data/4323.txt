Super Gem Fighter Mini Mix
Super Gem Fighter Mini Mix, released in Japan as , is a Japanese children's fighting video game released by Capcom in 1997 for the CPS II arcade system. It was ported to the PlayStation (which retained the "Pocket Fighter" title for its American and European release), Sega Saturn, and WonderSwan (in Japan only), as well as PlayStation 2 as a part of the "SFA"-themed compilation title, "Street Fighter Alpha Anthology".

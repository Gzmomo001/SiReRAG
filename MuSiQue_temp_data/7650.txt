Revolutionary Road (film)
In 1948, Frank Wheeler (DiCaprio) meets April (Winslet) at a party. He is a longshoreman, hoping to be a cashier; she wants to be an actress. Frank later secures a sales position with Knox Machines, for which his father worked for 20 years, and April and he marry. The Wheelers move to 115 Revolutionary Road in suburban Connecticut when April becomes pregnant.

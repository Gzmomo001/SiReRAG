Abdul Rahman bin Faisal
Abdul Rahman bin Faisal Al Saud (1845–1928) ( / ALA-LC: "‘Abd ar Raḥman bin Fayṣal") was the last ruler of the Emirate of Nejd, Second Saudi State. He was the youngest son of Faisal bin Turki bin Abdullah Al Saud and the father of Ibn Saud, who founded the modern Saudi Arabia.

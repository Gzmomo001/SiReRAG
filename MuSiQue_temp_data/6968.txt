VideoKids
VideoKids were a 1980s Euro disco/Italo disco duo from The Netherlands, formed in 1984 and led by Peter Slaghuis, who died in a car accident in 1991, and Bianca Bonelli, who also had a solo hit single called "Je veux l'amour". They were probably most famous for their "Woodpeckers from Space" song. They released two albums, "The Invasion of the Spacepeckers" in 1984 and "Satellite" in 1985.

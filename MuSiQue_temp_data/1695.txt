Kifisias railway station
Kifisias railway station () is a railway station located on Kifisias Avenue in Marousi, Athens, Greece. The station is served by Proastiakos trains. It is on the Athens Airport–Kiato railway line and was first opened on 30 July 2004. It is located in the middle of Motorway 6, at the interchange of Kifisias Avenue. the station is served by three trains per hour to the airport, two to Ano Liosia and one to Kiato.

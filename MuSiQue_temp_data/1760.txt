I Love Life, Thank You
I Love Life, Thank You is the sixth mixtape by American recording artist Mac Miller. In 2011, Miller started the "Road 2 a Million Fans" series, during which he released a new song for every 100,000 Twitter followers he accumulated. On October 14, 2011, he released "I Love Life, Thank You" upon reaching one million followers. Seven of the thirteen tracks on the mixtape had previously been released throughout the course of the series.

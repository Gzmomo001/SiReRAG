The Suite Life Sets Sail
"The Suite Life Sets Sail" is the pilot episode of the popular Disney Channel sitcom "The Suite Life on Deck", sequel and spin-off to the original series. Debby Ryan joins the cast as Bailey Pickett, close friends with Zack, girlfriend to Cody and roommate with London, as well as Ashley Tisdale leaving the main cast due to Maddie Fitzpatrick having to attend college.

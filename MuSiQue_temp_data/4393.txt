Tarrafal de São Nicolau (municipality)
Tarrafal de São Nicolau is a "concelho" (municipality) of Cape Verde. Situated in the western part of the island of São Nicolau, it covers 35% of the island area (119.8 km²), and is home to 41% of its population (5,237 at the 2010 census). Its seat is the town Tarrafal de São Nicolau.

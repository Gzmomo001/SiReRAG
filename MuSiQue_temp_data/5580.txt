Andy Blankenbuehler
Andy Blankenbuehler (born March 7, 1970) is an American dancer, choreographer and director primarily for stage and concerts. He has been nominated for the Tony Award for Best Choreography five times, and has won three times: for "In the Heights" (2008), "Hamilton" (2016), and "Bandstand" (2017). Blankenbuehler's other Broadway choreography work includes "9 to 5, Bring it On: The Musical", and the 2016 "Cats" revival. Blankenbuehler was awarded the Kennedy Center Honor in 2018. He is currently choreographing the movie version of "Cats".

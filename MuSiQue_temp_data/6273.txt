Titof
Titof was invited onto the set of a film by his friend Sebastian Barrio, a famous French porn actor. When the actor in a scene failed to perform, Titof was asked to take over. Titof plays in both straight and gay productions. In addition to pornographic films for heterosexual audiences, he has produced and directed two gay pornographic movies entitled "Titouch" and "Titouch 2", where he performed as both a top and as a bottom. He also performed in four other gay movies for Hervé Bodilis: "Christophe", "Titof, pompier en service" (Titof, fireman on duty), "Permission à Paris" (On leave in Paris), and "Titof and the College Boys".

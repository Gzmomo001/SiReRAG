Reach Out (Four Tops album)
Rounding out the LP are a pair of Monkees covers ("Last Train to Clarksville" and "I'm a Believer"), a cover of The Association's "Cherish", and the originals "Wonderful Baby" and "What Else Is There to Do (But Think About You)". "Reach Out" was followed one month later by a Four Tops' "Greatest Hits" album, which contained all of the group's hit singles to that point.

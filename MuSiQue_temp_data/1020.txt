Chasing Cars
The song was featured in the 2016 documentary Holy Hell about the 1980s West Hollywood cult Buddhafield and its allegedly manipulative and abusive leader. The song plays on toward the end of the documentary as ex-members of the cult are seen dancing in a field and while crying. The song is referenced by Ed Sheeran in his song ``All of the Stars ''. Grey's Anatomy Season 7, Episode 18,`` Song Beneath the Song'', featured characters singing ``Chasing Cars ''in a musical episode.

I Love Money
I Love Money is an American reality television series airing on VH1 and produced by 51 Minds Entertainment, a subsidiary of Endemol, and is a spin-off of "Flavor of Love", "Charm School", "I Love New York", "Rock of Love", "Real Chance of Love", "For the Love of Ray J", "Daisy of Love" and "Megan Wants a Millionaire". Former contestants on these shows compete in physical and mental challenges, aiming for a $250,000 grand prize. Created by executive producers Cris Abrego and Mark Cronin.

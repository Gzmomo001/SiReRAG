The Lawrence Welk Show
The Lawrence Welk Show is an American televised musical variety show hosted by big band leader Lawrence Welk. The series aired locally in Los Angeles for four years, from 1951 to 1955, then nationally for another 27 years on ABC from 1955 to 1971, and in first - run syndication from 1971 to 1982. Repeat episodes are broadcast in the United States by Public Broadcasting Service (PBS) stations. These airings incorporate an original program -- usually, a color broadcast from 1965 through 1982 -- in its entirety. In place of the commercials, newer performance and interview clips from the original stars and / or a family member of the performers are included; these clips are occasionally updated.

Dan Fitzsimons
Dan Fitzsimons (born February 26, 1969) is an American screenwriter who has written a miniseries and two television movies. He co-wrote the miniseries "Blackout" (2012) and wrote the TV movie "Desolation Canyon" (2006). He received the story credit for "Ring of Death" (2008) and was an associate producer for the horror film "The Season" (2008) which was an official selection at Screamfest 2008. Raised in Janesville, Wisconsin, Fitzsimons attended Joseph A. Craig High School.

Eho Sta Matia Ourano
Eho Sta Matia Ourano is an album by popular Greek artist Katy Garbi. It was released on 23 April 2005 by Sony BMG Greece and went Gold in Greece and Cyprus. Christos Dantis composed all the songs and also performs a duet in the song "Spaciba Baby". Katy Garbi promoted 3 of her songs on ANT1 show "Fame Story 3" before album's release. She performed the lead single "Akouse Agori Mou" on Mad Video Music Awards 2005 too. The track list contains laika and pop songs. "Eho Sta Matia Ourano" is Katy's last collaboration with her producer Giannis Doulamis, who was the producer of all her albums since her debut album "Prova".

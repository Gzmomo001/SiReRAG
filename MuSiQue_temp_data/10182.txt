David Lee (still photographer)
He is the younger brother of film director Spike Lee, and has done the still photography for all of his older brother's feature films with the exception of "Get on the Bus" and "He Got Game". Other films he has done still photography for include "The Preacher's Wife", "The Best Man", "Pollock", "Made", "Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind", "American Gangster" and the television series "The Wire".

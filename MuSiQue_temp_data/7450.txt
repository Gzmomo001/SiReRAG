Drentse Courant
The Drentse Courant is a former regional newspaper from the Netherlands. It was published from 1991 to 2002. It was a merger of the "Drentse en Asser Courant" (1961) en "Hoogeveens Dagblad" (1966), themselves also merged newspapers. In 2002, the "Drentse Courant" merged with the "Nieuwsblad van het Noorden" and the "Groninger Dagblad" into the "Dagblad van het Noorden".

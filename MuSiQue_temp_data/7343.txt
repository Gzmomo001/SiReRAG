Dan Angel
Dan Angel is an American film and television producer, screenwriter, story editor and showrunner. He has written and produced a number of television series and movies, often with his writing partner Billy Brown, including "The X-Files", "Goosebumps", "Animorphs", "", "Door to Door", "", "Christmas in Canaan", "" and "Dan Vs.".

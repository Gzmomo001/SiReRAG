Devara Naadalli
Devara Naadalli (, "In God's Own Country") is a 2016 Indian Kannada language political thriller film written and directed by B. Suresha, inspired by an article that appeared on "The Times of India" in 1998, of an incident that occurred in a town in coastal Karnataka. It features an ensemble cast of Prakash Raj, Achyuth Kumar, Mandya Ramesh and Sihi Kahi Chandru.

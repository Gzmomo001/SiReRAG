Bakhytzhan Zhumagulov
Bakhytzhan Zhumagulov (, "Baqytjan Tursynuly Jumaǵulov"; born 18 August 1953) is a Kazakh politician who was Chairman of Otan and after renamed Nur Otan, the largest political party in Kazakhstan. and acting chairman of Nur Otan party, created by merging a number of other parties into Otan.

McLeod's Daughters (season 5)
In the fifth season finale, Tess has a recurring dream about Nick. Meanwhile, Stevie tries to put off her feelings about Alex, but ca n't do it anymore, so she goes to lay it on the line. Alex and Sandra, however, suffer from Harry's dismissal of them and the moving on Nick, and the two end up in the shack Harry wants renovated to lure Sally back. The shack catches on fire and Stevie is there to save them, but the timing for Alex is n't right for Stevie to tell him her feelings. And, Jodi gets the DNA results back - she is Jack McLeod's daughter, but when she goes to tell Tess, Tess is sleeping. The two do not notice that Nick's watch has started to tick again.

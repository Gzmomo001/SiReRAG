The Fox in the Attic
"The Fox in the Attic" was originally published in 1961 by Chatto & Windus: London as v. 1 of "The Human Predicament" trilogy, and then in the United States by Harper & Brothers: New York. This was 23 years after Hughes's previous novel, "In Hazard: A Sea Story", and 33 years after "A High Wind in Jamaica", which was a best seller in the United Kingdom and America. It was published the following year in Sweden (Stockholm: Norstedt) as "Räven på vinden".

Sadie Plant
Sadie Plant left the University of Warwick in 1997 to write full-time. She published a cultural history of drug use and control, and a report on the social effects of mobile phones, as well as articles in publications as varied as the "Financial Times", "Wired", "Blueprint", and "Dazed and Confused". She published the book Z"eros + Ones" in 1997, in which she reveals how women's role in programming have been overlooked. She was interviewed as one of the 'People to Watch' in the Winter 2000–2001 issue of "Time".
